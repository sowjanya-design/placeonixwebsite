SAP & IT Training in Hyderabad — Your Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about our courses, placements and location in Hyderabad.

Which is the best SAP training institute in Hyderabad?

Placeonix is a trusted SAP and IT training institute in Hyderabad's Financial District, offering SAP BTP, CPI, CAPM, RAP, UI5/Fiori, ABAP on S/4HANA, FICO, MM, SD and BASIS. With small batches of maximum 30, live real-time projects, internship access and 100% placement assistance, learners get hands-on training aligned with real hiring needs.

Do you provide IT training with placement in Hyderabad?

Yes. Every Placeonix program includes complete placement assistance — resume building, mock interviews, LinkedIn optimisation, recruiter connects and job referrals with our partner companies. Top performers can secure internships even mid-programme.

What courses does Placeonix offer in Hyderabad?

We offer SAP modules (BTP, CPI, CAPM, RAP, UI5/Fiori, ABAP, FICO, MM, SD, BASIS), Data Science & Analytics, DevOps & Cloud Engineering, Full Stack Web Development, Java Backend, Cybersecurity Fundamentals and UI/UX Design, plus combo packages such as SAP Integration Expert and AI + Data Science.

Where is Placeonix located in Hyderabad?

Placeonix is located at Kapil Kavuri Hub, No. 144, 9th Floor, 152, Financial District, Hyderabad, Telangana 500032. We are open Monday to Saturday, 9 AM – 7 PM IST. Call +91 99494 94020 or +91 91217 59191.

Are the training programs suitable for freshers and working professionals?

Yes. Our SAP and IT courses are designed for freshers, career switchers and working professionals. Batches run 6 days a week with multiple timings per day, so you can learn alongside your job or studies.

Do you offer online or classroom SAP training in Hyderabad?

We offer instructor-led training with real system access, live projects and personalised mentoring. Contact our admissions team to discuss classroom and online options that fit your schedule.
