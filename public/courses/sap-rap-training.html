Introduction

What you learn in this SAP RAP course

The ABAP RESTful Application Programming Model (RAP) is SAP's strategic model for building modern OData v4 services and Fiori applications directly on the ABAP stack. As S/4HANA replaces classic ABAP development patterns, RAP has become the standard skill for on-stack and embedded extensibility.

This course takes you from CDS view modeling to full behavior-driven application development. You will define service bindings, build managed and unmanaged RAP Business Objects, implement validations, determinations and actions, handle drafts for transactional apps, and connect your services to Fiori Elements UIs — through guided, hands-on labs on a live S/4HANA system.

FAQs

SAP RAP Training — Frequently Asked Questions

What is SAP RAP?

SAP RAP (ABAP RESTful Application Programming Model) is SAP's modern framework for building OData v4 services and Fiori apps directly on the ABAP stack using behavior definitions and CDS views.

Who should take the SAP RAP course?

ABAP developers upgrading from classic development, SAP technical consultants, and Fiori/UI5 developers who need backend OData skills.

Do I need ABAP experience?

Yes, working knowledge of ABAP is expected since RAP is an ABAP-based framework.

How long is the SAP RAP training?

Typically 6–8 weeks with instructor-led sessions, hands-on labs and a capstone project.

Is it available online and classroom?

Yes — both instructor-led classroom training in Hyderabad and live online batches.

Will I get access to a real S/4HANA system?

Yes. Labs run in ABAP Development Tools against a live S/4HANA or ABAP Cloud-enabled system.

What topics are covered?

CDS view modeling, service bindings, behavior definitions and implementations, managed/unmanaged BOs, draft handling, validations, determinations, actions and Fiori Elements integration.

What is the difference between managed and unmanaged Business Objects?

Managed BOs let RAP generate standard create/update/delete persistence automatically; unmanaged BOs require you to implement all persistence logic yourself, typically for complex or legacy-integrated scenarios.

Are there hands-on projects?

Yes — a managed BO with draft handling, an unmanaged BO scenario, and a capstone OData v4 service with a Fiori Elements app.

Does it prepare me for SAP certification?

Yes. The curriculum aligns with SAP's ABAP Cloud / RAP-related associate certification topics, with guidance and practice questions. The certificate itself is issued by SAP.

What job roles can I target?

SAP RAP Developer, ABAP Cloud Developer, S/4HANA Technical Consultant and Fiori Backend Developer.

Is SAP RAP in demand?

Yes. RAP is SAP's strategic model for on-stack extensibility on S/4HANA and demand is rising as classic ABAP is phased out for new builds.

Do you provide placement assistance?

Yes — resume building, mock interviews, interview questions, LinkedIn optimisation and referrals to hiring partners.

What is the batch size?

Maximum 30 per batch, so every learner gets individual attention.

Can working professionals join?

Yes — weekend and evening batches are available.

Will I get a completion certificate?

Yes, a Placeonix course completion certificate, plus guidance toward the official SAP certification.

How is SAP RAP different from SAP CAPM?

RAP builds OData services inside the ABAP stack using ABAP Cloud for on-stack/embedded extensions on S/4HANA. CAPM builds services on SAP BTP using CDS with Node.js or Java for side-by-side extensions.

Yes — the SAP Fiori Full-Stack combo pairs RAP with SAP UI5/Fiori for end-to-end app development at a bundled price.
