Introduction

What you learn in this SAP SD course

SAP SD (Sales and Distribution) is the module that runs the order-to-cash process for organizations that sell products or services — from the moment a customer places an order through pricing, delivery, billing and cash collection. It is one of the most widely implemented SAP modules because every company with a sales function needs it.

This course takes you from organizational structure and master data to configuring the complete sales cycle. You will process sales orders, build pricing procedures using the condition technique, run availability checks, manage deliveries and billing, configure credit management, and understand how SD changes in S/4HANA with Fiori apps and Advanced ATP. Every concept is reinforced with lab work so you leave with practical, demonstrable experience.

Curriculum

Detailed SAP SD curriculum

Structured modules that build from organizational structure to full order-to-cash configuration.

Week 1 SD Organizational Structure & Master Data
  • Sales organization, distribution channel, division structure
  • Sales areas, sales offices and sales groups
  • Customer master and material master for sales
  • Condition master data and customer-material info records
Week 2 Sales Order Processing
  • Sales document structure: header, item, schedule line
  • Sales order types and their configuration
  • Item categories and item category determination
  • Schedule line categories and copy control
Week 3 Pricing Procedures & Condition Technique
  • Condition tables, access sequences and condition types
  • Pricing procedure determination and configuration
  • Discounts, surcharges, taxes and freight conditions
  • Condition exclusion and pricing analysis
Week 4 Availability Check & Delivery Processing
  • Availability check (ATP) configuration and control
  • Delivery document creation and processing
  • Picking, packing and goods issue
  • Route determination and shipping point rules
Week 5 Billing & Invoicing
  • Billing document types and billing plans
  • Invoice creation, cancellation and copy control
  • Intercompany billing and revenue account determination
  • Integration with FICO for financial postings
Week 6 Credit Management & Output Determination
  • Credit management configuration and credit checks
  • Blocked order release and credit exposure
  • Output determination for order, delivery and invoice
  • Output types, condition records and message control
Week 7 S/4HANA SD Changes & Capstone
  • Fiori apps for sales order and delivery management
  • Advanced ATP overview and simplified data model changes
  • Key differences from ECC SD
  • Capstone project and certification preparation

FAQs

SAP SD Training — Frequently Asked Questions

What is SAP SD?

SAP SD (Sales and Distribution) is the SAP module that manages the order-to-cash process — sales order processing, pricing, availability check, delivery, billing and credit management — for organizations that sell products or services.

Who should take the SAP SD course?

Commerce, business and engineering graduates, sales and customer service professionals, supply chain executives and functional consultants who want to specialize in SAP sales and order management.

Do I need prior sales or business experience?

A basic understanding of sales/order management concepts helps you move faster, but it is not mandatory. Business fundamentals are covered before SAP configuration.

How long is the SAP SD training?

Typically 6–8 weeks with instructor-led sessions, hands-on labs and a capstone project, depending on batch mode and pace.

Is it available online and classroom?

Yes — both instructor-led classroom training in Hyderabad and live online batches.

Will I get access to a real SAP system?

Yes. Labs run on a live SAP system covering sales order processing, pricing, delivery and billing.

What topics are covered?

SD organizational structure and master data, sales order processing, pricing and condition technique, availability check (ATP), delivery processing, billing, credit management, output determination and S/4HANA SD changes.

Which tools will I use?

SAP GUI, VA01/VA02/VA03, pricing condition technique, output determination, Fiori apps for sales, Advanced ATP, credit management and S/4HANA SD billing/invoicing.

Are there hands-on projects?

Yes — an end-to-end order-to-cash configuration, a pricing and credit management scenario, and an integration project with SAP MM/FICO, giving you portfolio-ready work.

Does it prepare me for SAP certification?

Yes. The curriculum aligns with the SAP Certified Application Associate - SAP S/4HANA Sales certification path, with guidance and practice questions. The certificate itself is issued by SAP.

What job roles can I target?

SAP SD Associate Consultant, SAP SD Consultant, Senior SAP SD Consultant, SAP Order Management Analyst and S/4HANA Order Management Architect.

Is SAP SD in demand?

Yes. Order-to-cash digitization and ongoing S/4HANA rollouts across retail, manufacturing and distribution are driving steady demand for SD consultants.

Do you provide placement assistance?

Yes — resume building, mock interviews, interview questions, LinkedIn optimisation and referrals to hiring partners.

What is the batch size?

Maximum 30 per batch, so every learner gets individual attention and feedback.

Can working professionals join?

Yes — weekend and evening batches are available.

Will I get a completion certificate?

Yes, a Placeonix course completion certificate, plus guidance toward the official SAP certification.

How is SAP SD different from SAP MM?

SD manages the sales and distribution / order-to-cash process — the sell side — covering sales orders, pricing, delivery and billing. MM manages procurement and inventory — the buy side — covering purchase orders and vendor invoices. Both integrate closely with FICO for financial postings.

Fees vary by batch, mode and combo package. Contact admissions for the current SAP SD fee and offers.

Yes — the SAP Logistics combo pairs SD with MM for a complete order-to-cash and procure-to-pay view at a bundled price.
