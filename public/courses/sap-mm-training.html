Introduction

What you learn in this SAP MM course

SAP Materials Management is the backbone of every SAP logistics landscape, governing how organisations procure, receive, store and pay for the materials and services they need. As supply chains digitize and enterprises roll out S/4HANA, MM remains one of the most consistently hired functional modules in the SAP ecosystem.

This course takes you from procurement fundamentals to configuring and running full materials management processes. You will set up organizational structures, process purchase requisitions and orders, manage inventory movements, run MRP, configure pricing procedures and perform invoice verification. Every concept is reinforced with lab work on a real SAP system so you leave with practical, demonstrable experience.

Curriculum

Detailed SAP MM curriculum

Structured modules that build from procurement fundamentals to S/4HANA MM innovations.

Week 1 Procurement Cycle & Organizational Structure
  • Procure-to-pay process overview
  • Enterprise structure: plant, storage location, purchasing organization
  • Material master and vendor master fundamentals
  • Number ranges and document types
Week 2 Purchase Requisitions & Purchase Orders
  • Creating and releasing purchase requisitions
  • Purchase order types and release strategies
  • Contracts, scheduling agreements and RFQs
  • Source determination and quotation comparison
Week 3 Inventory Management
  • Goods receipt and goods issue processes
  • Stock types: unrestricted, blocked, quality inspection
  • Movement types and physical inventory
  • Stock transfers and transfer postings
Week 4 Vendor Evaluation & Source Determination
  • Vendor evaluation criteria and scoring
  • Source list and quota arrangements
  • Purchase info records
Week 5 Material Requirements Planning (MRP)
  • MRP types and procedures
  • Running MRP and evaluating results
  • Reorder point and forecast-based planning
  • Stock/requirements list analysis
Week 6 Pricing Procedure & Condition Technique
  • Condition types, records and schemas
  • Pricing procedure determination
  • Discounts, surcharges and taxes
Week 7 Invoice Verification & S/4HANA MM
  • MIRO invoice verification and GR/IR clearing
  • Blocked invoices and payment processing
  • S/4HANA MM Fiori apps
  • MM in embedded analytics and capstone project

FAQs

SAP MM Training — Frequently Asked Questions

What is SAP MM?

SAP Materials Management (MM) is the SAP module that manages procurement, purchasing and inventory processes — covering purchase requisitions, purchase orders, goods movements, invoice verification and material valuation.

Who should take the SAP MM course?

Supply chain, mechanical and industrial engineering graduates, commerce graduates, and working professionals in procurement, purchasing or logistics who want to move into SAP consulting roles.

Do I need prior supply chain experience?

Not strictly. A basic understanding of procurement and inventory concepts helps, but the course covers these fundamentals from the ground up.

How long is the SAP MM training?

Typically 6–8 weeks with instructor-led sessions, hands-on labs and a capstone project, depending on batch mode and pace.

Is it available online and classroom?

Yes — both instructor-led classroom training in Hyderabad and live online batches.

Will I get access to a real SAP system?

Yes. Labs run on a live SAP system with GUI and Fiori access, so you configure and run real MM transactions.

What topics are covered?

Procurement cycle fundamentals, organizational structure, purchase requisitions and orders, inventory management, vendor evaluation, MRP, pricing procedures, invoice verification and S/4HANA MM innovations.

Which tools will I use?

SAP GUI, MRP, Purchase Info Records, Inventory Management, Fiori apps for MM, Pricing Procedures, Vendor Master, S/4HANA MM, Batch Management and Output Determination.

Are there hands-on projects?

Yes — a procure-to-pay cycle configuration, an MRP and inventory optimization scenario, and integration with SD/FICO, giving you portfolio-ready work.

Does it prepare me for SAP certification?

Yes. The curriculum aligns with the SAP Certified Application Associate - SAP S/4HANA Sourcing and Procurement path, with guidance and practice questions. The certificate itself is issued by SAP.

What job roles can I target?

SAP MM Associate Consultant, SAP MM Consultant, SAP MM/SCM Support Consultant, SAP Procurement Consultant and Senior SAP MM Consultant.

Is SAP MM in demand?

Yes. Supply chain digitization and ongoing S/4HANA rollouts across manufacturing, retail and logistics keep demand strong for MM skills.

Do you provide placement assistance?

Yes — resume building, mock interviews, interview questions, LinkedIn optimisation and referrals to hiring partners.

What is the batch size?

Maximum 30 per batch, so every learner gets individual attention and feedback.

Can working professionals join?

Yes — weekend and evening batches are available.

Will I get a completion certificate?

Yes, a Placeonix course completion certificate, plus guidance toward the official SAP certification.

How does SAP MM differ from SAP SD?

MM handles procurement and inventory — the buy side of the business. SD handles sales and distribution — the sell side. Both integrate closely for procure-to-pay and order-to-cash flows.

What is the course fee?

Fees vary by batch, mode and combo package. Contact admissions for the current SAP MM fee and offers.

Do you offer a combo with SAP SD?

Yes — the SAP Logistics Expert combo pairs MM with SD for an end-to-end logistics view at a bundled price.
