Introduction

What you learn in this UI/UX Design course

Every digital product needs someone who can turn user needs into usable, well-crafted interfaces. This course starts with the design thinking process and user research methods — interviews, personas and empathy maps — before moving into information architecture, wireframing and Figma.

From there you build visual design skills (typography, colour theory, spacing and hierarchy), learn to build and use design systems, and create interactive prototypes. The course closes with usability testing, a mobile app project, a website project, and a portfolio case study you can present to employers.

Curriculum

Detailed UI/UX Design curriculum

Structured modules that build from design thinking to a portfolio-ready case study.

Week 1 Design Thinking & User Research
  • Design process and problem discovery
  • User-centered design and case studies
  • User interviews, personas and empathy maps
  • Research documentation
Week 2 Information Architecture & Wireframing
  • User flows, site maps and task flows
  • Low-fidelity wireframes
  • Layout principles and mobile-first thinking
Week 3 Figma Basics
  • Frames and tools
  • Components and auto layout basics
  • Hands-on Figma practice
Week 4 Visual Design & Design Systems
  • Typography, colour theory, spacing and hierarchy
  • Components, variants and style guides
  • Reusable design patterns
Week 5 Prototyping & Usability Testing
  • Interactive prototypes and micro-interactions
  • Testing methods and feedback analysis
  • Iteration process
Week 6 Mobile App & Website Projects
  • Mobile app concept, wireframes, UI screens and prototype
  • Responsive landing page and website design
Week 7 Client Simulation & Portfolio
  • Requirement gathering and design delivery simulation
  • Case study structure and problem-solution storytelling
  • Behance/portfolio setup
Week 8 Interview & Placement Readiness
  • UX interview questions and portfolio presentation
  • Design task approach and mock interviews
  • LinkedIn profile and application strategy

FAQs

UI/UX Design Training — Frequently Asked Questions

What does the UI/UX Design course cover?

Design thinking, user research, information architecture, wireframing, Figma, visual design, design systems, prototyping and usability testing.

Do I need a design background to join?

No. The course is built for beginners with a creative interest and basic computer knowledge, progressing to intermediate skills.

How long is the training?

Approximately 2–4 months, from research through portfolio finalisation and interview preparation.

Will I build a design portfolio?

Yes — a mobile app project and a website project, packaged into a case-study driven portfolio.

Which tools will I learn?

Figma is the primary tool, alongside wireframing, prototyping and design system practices.

Do you provide placement assistance?

Yes — portfolio review, mock interviews, UX interview preparation, LinkedIn optimisation and referrals to hiring partners.

Is the course available online?

Yes, both classroom training in Hyderabad and live online batches are available.

What is the batch size?

Maximum 30 per batch, so every learner gets individual attention and feedback.
