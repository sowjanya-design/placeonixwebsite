Introduction

What you learn in this SAP FICO course

SAP FICO is one of the most widely implemented modules in the SAP ecosystem, forming the financial backbone of every SAP-run enterprise. As organisations migrate to S/4HANA Finance and Central Finance, the need for consultants who understand both accounting processes and SAP configuration has never been higher.

This course takes you from core accounting concepts to full module configuration. You will configure General Ledger and New GL, set up Accounts Payable and Accounts Receivable, manage Asset Accounting and Bank Accounting, and work through Controlling processes including cost center accounting, internal orders, profit center accounting, product costing and profitability analysis. Every concept is reinforced with lab work on a live system so you leave with practical, demonstrable experience.

Curriculum

Detailed SAP FICO curriculum

Structured modules that build from core Finance to full Controlling and month-end close.

Week 1 General Ledger (GL) Accounting & New GL
  • Chart of accounts, GL master data and account groups
  • Document types, posting keys and document splitting
  • New GL concepts, ledger groups and parallel ledgers
  • Financial statement versions and reporting
Week 2 Accounts Payable (AP) & Accounts Receivable (AR)
  • Vendor and customer master data
  • Invoice processing, payment terms and payment runs
  • Dunning, credit management and open item clearing
  • Integration with Materials Management and Sales & Distribution
Week 3 Asset Accounting (AA)
  • Asset classes, master data and depreciation areas
  • Acquisitions, transfers and retirements
  • Depreciation runs and year-end closing
Week 4 Bank Accounting & Integration
  • House banks, bank master data and reconciliation accounts
  • Electronic bank statement processing
  • Bank Communication Management and payment integration
Week 5 Controlling — Cost Center Accounting & Internal Orders
  • Cost center hierarchies and cost element accounting
  • Planning, allocations and assessment cycles
  • Internal order creation, settlement and reporting
Week 6 Profit Center Accounting & Product Costing
  • Profit center hierarchies and derivation
  • Product cost planning and cost estimates
  • Cost object controlling and variance analysis
Week 7 Profitability Analysis (CO-PA) & Month-End Close
  • Costing-based and account-based CO-PA
  • Value fields, characteristics and reporting
  • Month-end close activities and capstone project

FAQs

SAP FICO Training — Frequently Asked Questions

What is SAP FICO?

SAP FICO is the Finance (FI) and Controlling (CO) module of SAP used for external financial accounting, statutory reporting and internal management/cost accounting — covering General Ledger, AP, AR, Asset Accounting, Bank Accounting and Controlling.

Who should take the SAP FICO course?

Commerce, finance and accounting graduates, CA/CMA aspirants, working finance professionals and consultants who want to move into SAP finance implementation and support roles.

Do I need a finance or accounting background?

Basic accounting/finance knowledge is recommended since FICO configuration builds on core accounting concepts. Fundamentals are covered for those who need a refresher.

How long is the SAP FICO training?

Typically 6–8 weeks with instructor-led sessions, hands-on labs and a capstone project, depending on batch mode and pace.

Is it available online and classroom?

Yes — both instructor-led classroom training in Hyderabad and live online batches.

Will I get access to a real SAP system?

Yes. Labs run on a live SAP S/4HANA Finance environment covering GL, AP/AR, Asset Accounting and Controlling.

What topics are covered?

General Ledger and New GL, AP/AR, Asset Accounting, Bank Accounting, cost center accounting, internal orders, profit center accounting, product costing, CO-PA and month-end close.

Which tools will I use?

SAP GUI, S/4HANA Finance, Fiori apps for Finance, New GL, CO-PA, Asset Accounting, Bank Communication Management, SAP Analytics Cloud and Central Finance.

Are there hands-on projects?

Yes — GL/AP/AR configuration, a costing and profitability analysis scenario, and a finance close simulation, giving you portfolio-ready work.

Does it prepare me for SAP certification?

Yes. The curriculum aligns with the SAP Certified Application Associate - SAP S/4HANA Finance path, with guidance and practice questions. The certificate itself is issued by SAP.

What job roles can I target?

SAP FICO Consultant, SAP S/4HANA Finance Consultant, Finance Functional Analyst, SAP Support Consultant and SAP Finance Business Analyst.

Is SAP FICO in demand?

Yes. S/4HANA Finance and Central Finance migrations, plus growth of GCCs in India, are driving strong, sustained demand for FICO consultants.

Do you provide placement assistance?

Yes — resume building, mock interviews, interview questions, LinkedIn optimisation and referrals to hiring partners.

What is the batch size?

Maximum 30 per batch, so every learner gets individual attention and feedback.

Can working professionals join?

Yes — weekend and evening batches are available.

Will I get a completion certificate?

Yes, a Placeonix course completion certificate, plus guidance toward the official SAP certification.

How does FICO differ from CO alone, and how do FI and CO relate?

FI handles external financial accounting and statutory reporting (GL, AP, AR, Asset Accounting), while CO handles internal management and cost accounting (cost centers, internal orders, profitability analysis). Both are tightly integrated within the FICO module.
