Introduction

What you learn in this combo

Real SAP business processes rarely stay inside one module — procurement (MM) and sales (SD) both post accounting entries into finance (FICO). This combo builds each module individually and then connects them: FI/CO foundations, GL/AP/AR/asset processes, the full procurement cycle in MM, the full order-to-cash cycle in SD, and the integration points between all three.

Beyond configuration knowledge, you practise the analyst side of the role — requirement gathering, writing functional specifications, test scenarios, UAT support and production ticket handling — through case studies that mirror real client engagements.

Curriculum

Detailed SAP Functional Analyst curriculum

24 weeks combining SAP FICO, MM and SD with cross-module integration and analyst skills, each module labelled by sub-course focus.

Week 1 SAP Foundation
  • SAP overview, ERP business processes and navigation
  • Consultant role overview
Week 2-3 SAP FICO Finance Foundation & Configuration
  • FI process, GL/AP/AR overview and accounting documents
  • Company code, chart of accounts, posting period
Week 4-5 SAP MM Procurement Foundation & Integration
  • Procurement cycle, material/vendor master, purchase order process
  • Goods receipt, invoice verification, MM-FI integration
Week 6-7 SAP SD Sales Foundation & Integration
  • Sales process, customer master, sales order
  • Delivery and billing, SD-FI integration, order-to-cash case study
Week 8 Cross-Module Integration
  • P2P and O2C business document flow
  • Integration points and scenario mapping
Week 9-12 Functional Analyst Skills
  • Requirement gathering, stakeholder questions, gap analysis
  • Functional specification structure, process flow diagrams
  • Testing, UAT support and support process (tickets, RCA)
Week 13-17 Case Studies, Reporting & Communication
  • P2P and O2C mini case studies with documentation
  • Standard reports and business KPI writing
  • Client communication and project methodology overview
Week 18-20 Capstone Project
  • Business scenario selection and process mapping
  • Business requirement document, functional spec and test scenarios
  • Integration flow, issue handling and documentation review
Week 21-24 Interview & Placement Readiness
  • FICO/MM/SD scenario questions and functional analyst interview questions
  • Project explanation, client scenario questions and mock interviews
  • Resume, project summary, LinkedIn profile and HR preparation
