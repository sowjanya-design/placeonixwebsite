Introduction

What you learn in this SAP BASIS course

SAP BASIS is the technical backbone that keeps every SAP landscape — ECC, S/4HANA, BW, CRM and more — installed, connected, secure and performing well. Every organisation running SAP needs administrators who can manage transports, users, authorizations and system health, which makes BASIS one of the most stable and consistently in-demand SAP career paths.

This course takes you from landscape fundamentals to real administration tasks. You will install and configure system clients, set up the Transport Management System, create users and authorization roles, monitor system performance, schedule and troubleshoot background jobs, and get hands-on with HANA administration basics. Every concept is reinforced with lab work on a live SAP system so you leave with practical, demonstrable experience.

Curriculum

Detailed SAP BASIS curriculum

Structured modules that build from installation fundamentals to full landscape administration.

Week 1 Installation & Landscape Setup
  • SAP system architecture and landscape components
  • Installation process, kernel, instances and profiles
  • Application server vs database server roles
  • OS and database basics for BASIS admins
Week 2 Client Administration
  • Client creation, copy and deletion
  • Client settings and change control
  • System refresh concepts
  • Logical system configuration basics
Week 3 Transport Management System (TMS)
  • TMS configuration (STMS) and transport domains
  • Transport requests, routes and import queues
  • Change management and transport strategy
  • Troubleshooting failed transports
Week 4 User & Authorization Administration
  • User master maintenance (SU01)
  • Roles, profiles and authorization objects (PFCG)
  • Composite roles and mass user management
  • Security audit and SoD basics
Week 5 System Monitoring & Performance Tuning
  • Work processes, memory and buffer monitoring (SM50/SM66)
  • Performance analysis and tuning techniques
  • System logs and short dump analysis (ST22, SM21)
  • Alerting with the monitoring cockpit (RZ20)
Week 6 Background Jobs, Patching & Upgrades
  • Scheduling and monitoring background jobs (SM37)
  • Support pack stack and kernel patching
  • Upgrade planning and cutover basics
  • Backup and system recovery concepts
Week 7 HANA Administration Basics & Capstone
  • HANA Cockpit navigation and system monitoring
  • HANA backup, recovery and user management basics
  • Capstone landscape project and certification preparation

FAQs

SAP BASIS Training — Frequently Asked Questions

What is SAP BASIS?

SAP BASIS is the technical administration layer of SAP systems — covering installation, configuration, transport management, user/authorization administration, monitoring, performance tuning and upgrades.

Who should take the SAP BASIS course?

IT administrators, system engineers, Linux/Windows/database administrators and freshers from computer science backgrounds moving into SAP technical roles.

Do I need prior SAP experience?

No. Training starts from SAP landscape fundamentals. Prior OS/DB administration exposure helps but is not mandatory.

How long is the SAP BASIS training?

Typically 2–3 months with instructor-led sessions, hands-on labs and a capstone project, depending on batch mode and pace.

Is it available online and classroom?

Yes — both instructor-led classroom training in Hyderabad and live online batches.

Will I get access to a real SAP system?

Yes. Labs run on a live SAP system covering client admin, TMS, user management and monitoring.

What topics are covered?

Installation and landscape architecture, client administration, TMS, user/authorization administration, monitoring and performance tuning, background jobs, patching/upgrades, and HANA administration basics.

Which tools will I use?

SAP GUI, Solution Manager, HANA Cockpit, and transactions like STMS, SU01, PFCG, SM50/SM66, ST22, SM37 and RZ20.

Are there hands-on projects?

Yes — a transport landscape setup, a security/authorization design project, and an end-to-end monitoring capstone.

Does it prepare me for SAP certification?

Yes. The curriculum aligns with SAP Technology Associate / BASIS administration certification topics, with guidance and practice questions. The certificate itself is issued by SAP.

What job roles can I target?

SAP BASIS Consultant, SAP System Administrator, SAP Technical Support Engineer, SAP HANA Administrator and SAP Landscape Architect.

Is SAP BASIS in demand?

Yes. Every SAP landscape needs BASIS administrators, and S/4HANA/RISE migrations are increasing demand for HANA-savvy BASIS talent.

Do you provide placement assistance?

Yes — resume building, mock interviews, interview questions, LinkedIn optimisation and referrals to hiring partners.

What is the batch size?

Maximum 30 per batch, so every learner gets individual attention and feedback.

Can working professionals join?

Yes — weekend and evening batches are available.

Will I get a completion certificate?

Yes, a Placeonix course completion certificate, plus guidance toward the official SAP certification.

How is SAP BASIS different from other SAP modules?

Functional modules like FICO or MM configure business processes; BASIS is the technical layer — installation, transports, security and performance — that keeps every module running.

What is the course fee?

Fees vary by batch, mode and combo package. Contact admissions for the current SAP BASIS fee and offers.

Do you offer a combo with other SAP modules?

Yes — combo packages pairing BASIS with functional or technical modules are available at a bundled price.
